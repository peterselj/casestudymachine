Case Study Machine

Paste a product management case study prompt, add the data, and get back a deck you can present and prep notes that explain every call, the weak spots, and the questions you'll be asked.

You present the work, so you still have to understand it and agree with it. The prep notes are there for that. Check whether the prompt allows AI before you use this.
